Terms of Use — Lectio Bible

Updated: 2026-11-09

1. Service

Lectio Bible supports English Bible reading, vocabulary study and personal reflection. The current release is free and does not require an account. Some features need an internet connection or a compatible device speech service.

2. Bible text and learning material

The core English Bible versions are the Berean Standard Bible (BSB) and World English Bible (WEB), distributed as public-domain texts. Nothing in these terms removes the rights granted by their public-domain status.

LEE is an optional AI adaptation of BSB for learning. It is not an independently established Bible translation. AI-assisted explanations, translations and vocabulary material may contain mistakes. Compare important passages with established Bible translations and report errors to young@tellet.com.

3. Personal records and backups

Keep backups of records you want to preserve. Clearing app storage, uninstalling, changing devices or importing a backup can affect local records. Third-party downloads, translation and online speech can change or become unavailable.

4. Payments and refunds

Bible reading and existing free features remain free. Optional Lectio Pro is a non-consumable one-time purchase for two additional reading themes and reading/prayer journal CSV export. It does not automatically renew. Your local Google Play price is shown before purchase. Restore on Android using the same Google Play account; internet is required to verify and restore access. Pending purchases do not unlock Pro. Refunds follow Google Play's process and applicable consumer law; refunded or revoked purchases may lose Pro access. These rights do not restrict use of public-domain Bible texts.

5. Responsible use

Use the app lawfully and respect the rights of others. Do not disrupt the service or attempt unauthorized access to other people's data. Public-domain Bible texts retain their public-domain status.

6. Changes and contact

Features and these terms may be updated. Material changes will be communicated through the app or its store listing where appropriate. Nothing here excludes rights that cannot lawfully be excluded. Contact ableleafstudio at young@tellet.com.
