Privacy Policy — Lectio Bible

Updated: 2026-11-09

1. Who operates this app

Lectio Bible is operated by Young Maeng (Tellet). Contact young@tellet.com about privacy or data requests. This policy covers the Android app com.bible.lectio.

2. Data stored on your device

The app stores Bible reading positions, reading and vocabulary progress, starred words or sentences, prayer entries, settings, an installation ID, and downloaded content on your device. An installation ID is a pseudonymous identifier; it does not itself contain your name.

The current Android release does not require an account and does not enable developer-operated cloud synchronization, PostHog analytics, or Sentry error reporting. It does not request advertising ID, location, contacts, camera or microphone access.

3. Online features and service providers

Bible downloads request content from GitHub/jsDelivr. Online translation sends the word or sentence being translated to Google Translate. When high-quality online speech is used, the text to be spoken is sent to Microsoft's speech service. Device speech may use the speech provider configured on your device.

These providers receive the requested content and normal network information, such as your IP address, and may process it under their own privacy policies. We do not control their retention periods. Avoid including private information in text submitted to online features. Reading already downloaded content does not require these online requests.

4. Purposes and disclosure

Local data supports reading, study, bookmarks, prayer records and app preferences. Network requests support downloads, translation and speech. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever information you include.

5. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them using the available app controls, clear the app's storage, or uninstall it. Device or operating-system backup settings may retain or restore app data. Exported backups remain wherever you save them and must be deleted separately.

For support correspondence or privacy requests, contact young@tellet.com. We retain support correspondence while needed to respond and for any applicable legal obligations. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

6. Payments

Payments are not enabled in the current release. If optional paid features are introduced, their price, scope and purchase conditions will be shown before payment and this policy will be updated. No card details are collected by the current app.

7. Updates

We will update this policy when the app's data practices change. The update date appears above. Any future activation of cloud storage, analytics, or payment processing requires a corresponding policy and store-disclosure update.
