Daniel’s Clock Privacy Policy

ableleafstudio · 2026-11-18

Daniel’s Clock is an app by ableleafstudio for learning to read a clock. The app does not require an account or use ads or analytics.

The app or browser stores language preferences and learning progress on the device. ableleafstudio does not send these records to a server. Device backups follow the operating system settings.

Bundled audio plays offline. The operating system or browser can read some sentences aloud. The Android app selects voices available for offline playback. Speech processing on the web follows your browser and voice provider settings.

To delete your preferences and progress, clear the app’s storage in Android settings or clear the site’s data in your browser.

For privacy questions, email ableleafstudio@gmail.com. Your email address and message are kept for the time needed to respond and any period required by law.
